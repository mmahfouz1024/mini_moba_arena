# Privacy Policy — Legends Arena: Mini MOBA

Effective Date: 2026-11-18

**Legends Arena: Mini MOBA** ("the Game") respects your privacy.

## 1. Data Collection & Storage
- All player progress (rank points, wins, unlocked heroes, gold, and settings) is stored **locally on your device** via `SharedPreferences`.
- The Game does **not** require account registration and does **not** collect, transmit, or sell personally identifiable information.

## 2. Third-Party Services (Optional Future Integrations)
- In its base build, the Game contains **no third-party advertising, analytics, or external multiplayer server tracking**.
- If you later add Google AdMob or online multiplayer matchmaking before publishing, update this Privacy Policy and your Google Play Console Data Safety form accordingly.
